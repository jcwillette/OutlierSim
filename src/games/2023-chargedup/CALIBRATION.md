# Calibration: Charged Up (2023)

The owner asked for this comparison with real 2023 matches (ADR 0004 keeps it opt-in; ADR 0009 records the decision). The game was built blind first (`SOURCES.md`). Then its robots, human player stations and field rules were tuned so random alliances score like a real 2023 qualification match, and the tuned numbers became the season-to-season baselines in `.claude/skills/frc-game-from-manual/robot-baselines.md`.

- **Real data**: every official 2023 event on The Blue Alliance (61 regionals, 94 district events, 21 district championships and divisions, the Championship): 13,538 qualification matches, 27,076 alliance-matches with full score breakdowns, plus each event's component OPRs for robot tiers. Cached in `work/tba-cache/`, which isn't committed.
- **Not used**: match video. YouTube refused downloads from the cloud container ("confirm you're not a bot"), so action times couldn't be timed directly. Statbotics and match13 weren't needed.
- **Target**: the season's average qualification match (84.8 points per alliance without fouls), played by the menu's random preset (three different robot types per alliance) at normal difficulty, with breakdowns on.

- The real standard deviations pool events from week 1 to the Championship. Within one event, alliance scores spread with a standard deviation of 26 points (26.3 pooled), which the sim matches (25.8). Centered on the season mean, a typical event runs p10 51 / p25 66 / p50 84 / p75 103 / p90 119, against the sim's 55 / 67 / 85 / 107 / 121.
- **Sustainability** reaches 18 % against 24 % for the whole season. The season figure includes the district championships (69 %) and the Championship (66 %), where much stronger alliances played. At in-season events it was 14 %; an event as strong as the season mean (weeks 4-5) gave 19-23 %.
- **Fouls**: the sim calls only G207 and G202; real fouls cover every rule (extension, launching, over-capacity, …).
- **Parked robots** run high because the defender parks at the end instead of docking.

## What the engine couldn't express (now it can)

1. **Pickup time cost nothing.** A one-piece robot grabbed a piece on contact, and `intake.timeSec` only delayed the next pickup. It is now a timed grab: the robot stops that long once a piece is in reach (after a station's `handoffSec`).
2. **Every bot ran the planner's best auto**, scoring its preload and a staged piece. Real robots mostly score one preload or nothing. Robot types now have an `auto` routine: most pieces, whether to leave the starting zone, and the chance it runs.
3. **Every drive team was as quick as the bot difficulty.** Real cycles per teleop piece run from 16 s (top seventh) to over 60 s, mostly lining up, misses and retries. Robot types now have `alignSec`, the teleop line-up per pickup and placement. Programmed autos and the student's own robot don't pay it, and the bot difficulty scales it.
4. **Bots didn't build links** (1.4 per alliance from 12 pieces; real 2.5 from 11). `rules.ts` now tells bots, through the new `botValue` hook, that a node completing a link is worth 5 more. A pair that a third piece can finish is worth 1.5 more, and one of the first three co-op nodes 0.25 more. Real teams filled the co-op grid on purpose (73 % of matches).
5. **Long pickups made bots miss the endgame**: a bot started a 15 s line-up with 20 s left. Bots now don't start what they can't finish before leaving for their endgame action.

## What was tuned

Every robot type now stands for one seventh of real 2023 robots, ranked by contribution (component OPR per event, weighted by matches played). Drivetrains and mechanism times are the blind build's hardware estimates, unchanged. Human player stations are unchanged (`handoffSec` 1.5 s, shelves every 3 s, chute every 2.5 s, supply 16 / 14 / 12). Real results can't separate the human player's speed from the robot's, and the shelves were never the bottleneck at real scoring rates.

| Robot type (real tier)             | Line-up `alignSec` | Auto routine                 | Auto Charge Station (blind → now)         | Endgame, s (blind → now), success     | `reliability` (blind → now) |
| ---------------------------------- | ------------------ | ---------------------------- | ----------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------- | --------------------------- |
| Elevator all-rounder (top seventh) | 2.6 s              | 2 pieces, runs 85 %          | engage 4 → 5 s, 85 %; dock-only removed   | engage 4 → 6 (90 %), dock 2 → 3       | 0.97                        |
| Cone specialist (6th)              | 4.6 s              | 1 piece, 85 %                | dock 3 s → none                           | engage 5 → 7 (85 %), dock 2 → 3       | 0.95                        |
| Cube specialist (5th)              | 8.9 s              | 1 piece, 85 %                | engage 3.5 → 5 s, 80 %; dock-only removed | engage 3.5 → 6 (85 %), dock 2 → 3     | 0.95 → 0.92                 |
| Charge Station specialist (4th)    | 13.6 s             | 1 piece, doesn't leave, 80 % | engage 3 → 4 s, 85 %; dock-only removed   | engage 2.5 → 4 (90 %), dock 1.5 → 2.5 | 0.95 → 0.90                 |
| Mid-level arm (3rd)                | 12.1 s             | 1 piece, 65 %                | engage 5.5 s and dock 3 s → none          | engage 5.5 → 8 (70 %), dock 2.5 → 4   | 0.92 → 0.88                 |
| Kitbot (2nd)                       | 16.8 s             | 1 piece, 45 %                | dock 4 s → none                           | dock 3 → 8, 40 % per try              | 0.85 → 0.78                 |
| Defender (bottom seventh)          | 20.2 s             | none, 30 %                   | dock 3 s → none                           | dock 2 → none (parks)                 | 0.93 → 0.75                 |

A failed engage still docks (`fallback`). A failed dock docks nobody, and the robot tries again while there's time. The descriptions in `definition.ts` say each type's tier.

Field and rules: the Sustainability RP needs **5 links (4 with coopertition)**. The April 2023 manual (V4) says 6 (5), but every week-1-6 event and every district championship awarded it at 5 (4). Only the Championship used 6 (5). The blind build read the April copy, and its `SOURCES.md` had flagged this kind of later change as an open question. Every point value checks out against all 32,632 real breakdowns: fouls 5 / 12, links 5, mobility 3, park 2, dock / engage 6 / 10, and 8 / 12 in auto.

## Remaining gaps

- Per-action timing (substation, placing by row, balancing) isn't measured, only cycle totals. Video would split the line-up from the mechanism.
- Bots don't choose autos as an alliance (who docks, who scores two), so auto docks go to whichever capable robot claims the station.
- Defense is the defender preset plus opportunistic blocking; its real share and effect aren't in TBA data.
- Fouls: only G207 and G202 are called (0.72 of the real foul points).
- Robots don't tip cones, drop pieces or descore; all of that lives in the line-up time.

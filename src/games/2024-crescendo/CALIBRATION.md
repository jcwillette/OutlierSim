# Calibration: Crescendo (2024)

The 2024 game was first built blind from the kickoff manual (`SOURCES.md`, ADR 0004). On 25 November 2026 the owner gave permission to look past January 2024 (ADR 0012): Chief Delphi's strategy discussion and The Blue Alliance's results. They asked for the differences first, then to tune 2024 to match real life, to add the strategies and robot types teams really used, and to record every discrepancy, change and lesson. This file is that record. `SOURCES.md` keeps the blind build's reasoning; git history keeps the blind definition (before ADR 0012).

- **Real results**: every official 2024 event on The Blue Alliance (190: regionals, district events, district championships and their divisions, the Championship divisions and Einstein): 14,125 qualification matches, 28,250 alliance-matches with full score breakdowns, plus each event's component OPRs for robot tiers. Cached in `work/tba-cache/`, which isn't committed.
- **Strategy discussion**: about 18 Chief Delphi threads from 2024 (listed under "Real strategies" below), read through Chief Delphi's public JSON.
- **Not used**: match video (YouTube refuses downloads from the cloud container), Statbotics, match13.
- **Target**: the season's average qualification match (48.9 points per alliance without fouls), played by the menu's random preset at normal difficulty, with breakdowns on, the same target as 2023.

- The real standard deviations pool events from week 1 to the Championship. Within one event, alliance scores spread with a standard deviation of 18.8 points, which the sim's 15.4 approaches; the season-wide 24.2 also includes the spread between events.
- **AMPLIFIED NOTES** (0.72) are the biggest remaining gap: see "Remaining gaps".
- **Parked robots** run high (1.35): real robots that neither climbed nor parked (still cycling at the buzzer, lost, broken down) were 11 % of the top tier and 38 % of the bottom one; sim bots that can't climb always park. One point a robot.
- **Fouls** (0.40): the engine calls only G420, G422, G423 and G424. Real fouls cover every rule: G414 passes, over-extension, NOTE launching, more. 2023 had the same gap (0.72).
- **ENSEMBLE** (0.72): half the real in-season ENSEMBLE RPs (1,733 of 3,332) came from the opponent's G424 foul, not from the STAGE. Earned on the STAGE the RP was 7 % in-season and 11 % for the season; the sim earns it about as often and calls G424 a little less.

What the blind build got right: the score scale (0.92), auto (0.97), NOTES per tier (within 15 %, see below), LEAVE and MELODY. The 2023 line-up baselines carried over well. What it got wrong:

1. **The endgame** (1.50): climbing a chain was far harder than docking in 2023. The blind tiers climbed 44-88 % of matches; real tiers climbed 5-61 %. The blind stage specialist (88 %) had no real counterpart: no tier of real robots specialized in the STAGE.
2. **AMPLIFICATION** (0.43): bots shot as soon as they arrived, and the HUMAN PLAYER pressed for one robot. Real alliances held the bank and their shots for the press, and the 4-NOTE cap (Team Update 01, which the kickoff text lacked) made a press worth timing.
3. **Auto AMP NOTES** (21×): the planner valued banking the AMP in auto, real teams never did it.
4. **HARMONY, SPOTLIT and Coopertition**: three good-case guesses (two robots per chain, 25 % per HIGH NOTE throw, always cooperate) that real matches didn't bear out.
5. **No real strategies**: no passing, no tall robots, no SOURCE defense, every robot under the STAGE, and bots kept out of the opponent's STAGE ZONE all match.

## What the engine couldn't express (now it can, ADR 0012)

All opt-in: 2023 and the reference game play bit-for-bit as before (`npm run bench -- --compare`).

1. **Passing.** `GameDefinition.passing` (landing spots, the opponent's zones a pass may not come from, a minimum distance) and `shooter.passRange`: a robot lobs a held piece onto a spot on the floor for a teammate (`RobotCommand.pass`; the player's key is T, or the left stick press). The engine refuses passes outside teleop, from the forbidden zones (2024: the opponent's WING, G414) and from robots without a passing shooter.
2. **Feeders.** A specialist role with `feeds: true` fetches pieces far from its goal and passes them toward its teammates instead of carrying them, unless it is already within the passing `minDistance` (7 m). It leaves loose pieces near the landing spots to the robots it feeds.
3. **SOURCE defense.** A role with `defense: 'source'` plays defense from a post just outside the opponents' loading area instead of in front of their goal, and holds up robots on their way in as well as carriers on their way out (the Chief Delphi "Source Choke Point").
4. **Robots too tall for a structure.** `FieldElement.clearance`: robots taller than the STAGE core's 0.71 m treat it as a wall in their own nav grid (physics already did). The full-size robot drives around the STAGE; the SOURCE choke works against it.
5. **Late-match path zones.** `botHints.endgamePathZones`: zones bots path around only from 5 s before the endgame. 2024's opponent STAGE ZONE (G424 B) is now open until then, so robots drive under the opponent's STAGE on the way to and from the SOURCE, as real ones did.
6. **Holding a shot.** `GameRules.botHold`: the game tells bots a ready shot is about to be worth more. 2024 holds while the AMP bank is full (or a partner is about to fill it) and the HUMAN PLAYER is waiting; a bot holds at most 14 s per arrival.
7. **Station-only intakes.** `intake.floor: false` (2024's KitBot loaded through its launcher at the SOURCE).
8. **Robot shares.** `RobotArchetype.dealWeight`: how often the random preset deals a type (0: never). The two top-seventh builds are dealt 0.6 / 0.4, and the dedicated defender is no longer dealt.

## Remaining gaps

- **AMPLIFICATION** (0.72 of real AMPLIFIED NOTES): bots score the AMP about as often as real alliances (1.14) and press about as often, but get fewer NOTES into each press. Real alliances staged NOTES near the SPEAKER and in robots before pressing; bots fetch only after the press, and a feeder role is the only way they stock NOTES. The planner's AMP value is a knife edge against the free SPEAKER shot: tuning rounds gave an AMP share of 11 % with +1.2 and 30 % with +1.8.
- **Parking** (1.35): the sim's non-climbers always park; real ones often didn't.
- **Fouls** (0.40): only protected-zone contact and pinning are called; G414 isn't (bots never pass from the opponent's WING, and the player can't).
- **Event levels**: MELODY's district-championship and Championship thresholds, and the bigger auto and endgame of strong events, aren't modeled.
- **Defense's effect** and how often it was played aren't in TBA data; the size of the effect is a judgment call, as in 2023. Opponents don't steal passed NOTES on purpose.
- **The full-size robot's share** (0.4 of the top tier) is an estimate from Chief Delphi and the season's well-known robots; TBA can't tell designs apart.
- **Per-action timing** (SOURCE loading, AMP placement, shot time) isn't measured, only totals: no match video.

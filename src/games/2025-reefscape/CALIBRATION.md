# Calibration: Reefscape (2025)

The 2025 game was first built blind from the official materials as of January 2025 (`SOURCES.md`, ADR 0013). On 26 November 2026 the owner gave permission to look past January 2025 (ADR 0014): Chief Delphi's strategy discussion and The Blue Alliance's results. They asked to tune 2025 to match real life, to add the strategies and robot types teams really used, to log every discrepancy and change, and to record the lessons. This file is that record. `SOURCES.md` keeps the blind build's reasoning; git history keeps the blind definition (before ADR 0014).

- **Real results**: every official 2025 event on The Blue Alliance (203: regionals, district events, district championships and their divisions, the Championship divisions): 14,805 qualification matches, 29,610 alliance-matches with full score breakdowns. Robot tiers come from per-event least-squares contributions (OPR) computed from those matches, component by component. Cached in `work/tba-cache/`, which isn't committed.
- **Strategy discussion**: about 25 Chief Delphi threads from 2025 (listed under "Real strategies" below), read through Chief Delphi's public JSON.
- **Not used**: match video (YouTube refuses downloads from the cloud container), Statbotics, match13.
- **Target**: the season's average qualification match (95.8 points per alliance without fouls), played by the menu's random preset at normal difficulty with breakdowns on, the same target as 2023 and 2024.

## How TBA records 2025 (read before re-running)

- **The REEF**: `autoReef` is the REEF at the end of AUTO. `teleopReef` holds the BRANCHES at the end of the match, AUTO CORAL still on them included, and only the CORAL added to the trough in TELEOP. A CORAL scores once: AUTO CORAL still on a BRANCH earns no TELEOP points (checked against `teleopCoralPoints` in all 29,610 alliance-matches), which is how the sim scores too. `calibration.ts` counts TELEOP CORAL as what was added after AUTO.
- `teleopCoralCount` counts AUTO CORAL too at the Championship but not elsewhere, so the spec doesn't use it.
- `autoPoints` leaves ALGAE out: TBA puts every ALGAE, AUTO included, in `algaePoints`. The sim's AUTO points leave them out the same way.
- `coopertitionCriteriaMet` is per alliance: that alliance put 2 ALGAE in its PROCESSOR. Coopertition needs both alliances' flags (`CalibrationMetric.real` now gets the opponent's breakdown).
- `netAlgaeCount` counts robots' and HUMAN PLAYERS' ALGAE together.
- Einstein (the Championship finals) has no qualification matches.

What the blind build got right: the score scale (1.07), BARGE points (1.03), L3, the deep CAGE as the top tiers' endgame, the in-season RP thresholds, and the top tier's piece count (13.5 pieces against a real 13.7 CORAL and ALGAE). The 2023 line-ups with 2024's climb rates carried over to the top tiers. What it got wrong:

1. **The PROCESSOR** (4.65×) and **Coopertition** (98 % against 22 %). The HUMAN PLAYER's NET throw was guessed at 35 % ("Lessons from 2024": human-player feats run low); real HUMAN PLAYERS netted 86 % of what they got. So the bots saw a processed ALGAE as worth 6 - 1.4 points, where real teams saw 6 - 3.5 and processed only for Coopertition, which they agreed on in pairs. An engine artifact doubled it: bots keep their intake up, so a bot placing CORAL in front of a REEF face took that face's ALGAE without meaning to (also in AUTO).
2. **LEAVE** (0.70) and **the AUTO RP** (0.36). Robots whose auto routine failed sat still. Real robots drove forward even when they scored nothing: the bottom tier left the line 73 % of the time with 0.03 CORAL.
3. **Shallow CAGES** (25×). The middle tiers were built for the shallow CAGE; real robots built for the deep one or for none (1 % of robots hung from a shallow CAGE).
4. **AUTO by tier**: the low tiers scored too much in AUTO (0.3-0.6 CORAL against a real 0.03-0.23), the top tier too little (1.45 against 1.90).
5. **CORAL by level**: L2 0.50, L4 0.57, TELEOP CORAL points 0.67. The bots spent TELEOP on ALGAE, and never went for L2 unless the CORAL RP pulled them there.
6. **The CORAL RP** (0.24).
7. **Middle tiers that couldn't reach L4**: the blind pivot arm, tank-drive arm and trough-and-L2 scorer stopped at L3 or L2. Real tiers 3-5 put most of their CORAL on L4 ("every tier did every kind of scoring", 2024's lesson, again).

## What the engine couldn't express (now it can, ADR 0014)

All opt-in: 2023, 2024 and the reference game play bit-for-bit as before (`npm run bench -- --compare`, 8 matches each).

1. **Knocking a piece off a structure.** `HumanPlayerStation.knockOff`: a robot that reaches the piece can knock it to the floor instead of taking it (`RobotCommand.knock`, or automatically when it can't hold the piece). It falls beside the robot as a loose piece. Real robots "de-algaefied" most REEF ALGAE this way to open L2 and L3: alliances scored 2.9 of their 9 ALGAE but scored L3 in nearly every match.
2. **What clearing a station is worth.** `GameRules.botStationValue`: bots add it to taking the piece, and it is the only value of knocking it off. 2025 counts the BRANCHES a REEF ALGAE blocks.
3. **Loading on purpose.** `RobotCommand.loadFrom`: a piece resting on a structure goes only to a bot loading from that station, so a bot scoring beside it with its intake up doesn't sweep it up. Players (no `loadFrom`) take it as before.
4. **Drive-forward autos.** `AutoRoutine.driveOut`: when the routine doesn't run, the chance the robot still drives out of the starting zone, scoring nothing.
5. **Line-up per target.** `ScoringTarget.alignScale` multiplies the drive team's line-up for placements there: the trough (the face's whole width) 0.9, an L4 BRANCH (the top of a fully extended elevator) 1.2.
6. **Calibration metrics that see both alliances**: `CalibrationMetric.real(b, score, opp)` and `sim(s, opp)` (Coopertition).

## Remaining gaps

- **Parking** (PARK 1.16): climbers that fail end parked almost every time (real tiers 3-6: 46-65 % parked, 20-27 % did neither), and robots without a climber start long pickups in the last 30 s and miss the PARK (KitBot and low-resource robot: 37-41 % against a real 64-68 %). Fixing either changes the bots of every game.
- **Fouls** (0.51): only G421, G425, G427 and G428 are called.
- **L1 within tiers**: real middle and low tiers mixed L1 robots with elevators; the sim's elevators score little L1 and its three bottom types only L1.
- **The Championship thresholds** (7 CORAL per level, 16 BARGE points) and event levels aren't modeled.
- **Forced Coopertition** (scoring in the opponents' PROCESSOR), bump autos, and stealing the opponents' staged ALGAE on purpose aren't modeled.
- **Hard difficulty** over-nets ALGAE (above).
- **Defense's effect** isn't in TBA data; the size of the effect stays a judgment call, as in 2023 and 2024.
- **Per-action timing** (CORAL STATION loading, BRANCH placement, BARGE shots, climbs) isn't measured, only totals: no match video.
